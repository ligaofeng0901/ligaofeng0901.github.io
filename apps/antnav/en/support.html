FAQ

Does AntNav use GPS?

No. AntNav uses the accelerometer and magnetometer for local path integration (dead reckoning). It does not use Core Location or GPS.

Why does the app need Motion access?

Motion & Fitness access is required to detect steps and estimate heading. Without it, trail recording and return guidance cannot work. See the Privacy Policy.

How do I record a route?

Hold the iPhone roughly level with the top edge pointing forward. Tap Start, walk, then use the map to see your trail. Tap Reset or Restart to end a session.

How does Return mode work?

Tap Return. The orange arrow points toward your start (origin) relative to the phone’s heading. The compass also shows estimated distance and remaining steps based on your stride setting.

Why is accuracy imperfect?

Sensor-based dead reckoning drifts over time and distance. Magnetic interference indoors can affect heading. AntNav is best for short walks, indoor experiments, and educational demos—not long precision navigation.

Where is route history saved?

On your device only. Ending a session with Reset or Restart auto-saves a valid trail. Open History (clock icon, top-left) to review or swipe to delete.

Can I change stride length?

Yes. Open Settings (gear icon) to adjust stride length and step-detection sensitivity. Smaller strides (down to 0.10 m) can help younger walkers.

How do I delete my data?

Delete routes in History, or uninstall the app to remove on-device sandbox data.
