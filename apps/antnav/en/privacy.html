Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 16, 2026 · Applies to the iOS app AntNav (Bundle ID: com.ligaofeng0901.apps.antroute)

Summary: AntNav does not use GPS or location services. Motion sensor data is processed on your device for navigation. Route history stays on your iPhone. We do not run a developer backend that collects your personal data, and we do not require an account.

1. Who we are

AntNav is a GPS-free “ant-style” navigation experiment. Developer: ligaofeng0901. Contact details are on the Support page.

2. Data we process on device

AntNav uses on-device sensors to provide its features:

  • Accelerometer — to detect steps and estimate movement.
  • Magnetometer / device motion — to estimate phone heading.

These signals are processed locally on your iPhone to record a trail relative to your start point, guide you back toward the origin, and estimate distance and remaining steps.

3. Data stored on your device

When you end a recording (Reset or Restart), AntNav may save route history locally, including:

  • Timestamps
  • Path coordinates in a local origin-based system (not GPS latitude/longitude)
  • Step count, distance, and stride settings for that session

You can delete saved routes in the in-app History list. Uninstalling the app removes on-device sandbox data (subject to any device backups you manage).

4. System permissions

Motion & Fitness

Required for step detection and heading used in path integration. If access is denied, core navigation features will not work.

Location

AntNav does not request or use Core Location / GPS.

5. How we use data

  • Only to provide on-device navigation, trail recording, return guidance, and local history.
  • Not for advertising, sale of personal data, or cross-app tracking.

6. What we do not collect

  • Name, email, or account credentials (no login)
  • Precise or approximate GPS location
  • Contacts, photos, microphone, or camera data
  • Advertising identifiers for tracking
  • Analytics or crash telemetry via third-party SDKs (as of the current version)

7. Third parties

The current version relies on Apple system frameworks (Core Motion and related APIs). There are no additional analytics or ads SDKs. Apple may process information under Apple’s policies when you download the app or use system services. If our practices change, we will update this policy and App Store privacy labels.

8. Retention and deletion

  • Delete routes inside History in the app.
  • Uninstalling removes local app data on the device.
  • Because we do not hold a developer-side cloud copy of your routes, we generally cannot remotely erase your data for you.

9. Children

AntNav does not knowingly collect personal information from children. A smaller stride setting may be used by younger walkers; related data remains on-device.

10. Changes

We may update this policy and will revise the effective date on this page when we do.

11. Contact

Privacy questions: see Support or email ligaofeng0901@gmail.com.
