FAQ

How do I add a ticket?

On Timeline, tap add, then take a photo or choose from your library. On-device OCR fills fields when possible; edit and save.

Why does the app need Photos access?

To import ticket images and (optionally) scan your album for likely stubs. Recognition stays on your device and is not uploaded to a developer server. See the Privacy Policy.

Why Location?

Only to optionally tag where you watched a movie. Denying location does not block saving tickets or OCR.

Library scan is slow or stops in background?

Scan uses accurate on-device OCR, which is heavy on large libraries. iOS may limit background time; return to the foreground or leave the device idle/charging to continue. You can pause anytime in Settings.

What does iCloud sync do?

It merges your ticket archive across your Apple devices via your own iCloud Drive (photos optional). Sign in to iCloud on the device. The developer cannot read your sync files.

OCR got a field wrong

Edit fields on the ticket detail/editor screen. Candidates from library scan require your confirmation before import.

How do I delete my data?

Delete tickets in the app. If you used iCloud sync, also manage copies in iCloud Drive / Files. Uninstalling clears the on-device sandbox.
